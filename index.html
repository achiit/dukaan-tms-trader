Started with one gift that was hard to find

In 2023, my cousin’s wedding gave me the idea. We spent days going shop to shop for one good gift, and nothing felt both thoughtful and fairly priced. That week I decided to build the place I wished existed.

TMS Traders began from Pul Pehladpur with three partners who trusted the idea. Today we handle corporate gifting, wholesale and event décor for offices and families across Delhi-NCR and beyond. Our tagline, “That’s Make Smile”, is how we judge every order.
